Logistics Notifier

Logistics Notifier is an internal desktop tool used by the logistics team of PTSC Mechanical & Construction (PTSC M&C) to prepare routine shipment notification emails.

What the app does

  • Reads the project's logistics shipment report from Google Sheets (read-only access), using the Google account of the person running the tool.
  • Prepares draft Arrival Notice emails for shipments arriving at the yard, and weekly logistics report emails, in the user's own Microsoft Outlook.
  • Does not modify or delete any spreadsheet, and does not send data to any server other than Google's own API.

The tool is used only by authorised PTSC M&C staff on spreadsheets that have been shared with them.
